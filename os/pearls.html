Reusable teaching

Teaching pearls

Short facilitator moves that improve reasoning, communication, and transfer.

Clinical reasoning

Force the consequence

After a learner names a finding, ask what it changes. If nothing changes, ask whether the finding belongs in the focused examination.

Feedback

Repeat the rep

Feedback without another attempt tests memory, not performance. Make the next attempt short enough to happen immediately.

Communication

Replace labels with observations

When a report uses a vague label, ask what the learner saw, heard, or tested that supports it.

Pearls should be brief enough to use in the room.
