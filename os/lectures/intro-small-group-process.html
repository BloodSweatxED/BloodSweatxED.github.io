One-hour facilitator guide

Intro to Small-Group Process

Build a group that can think aloud, disagree productively, and notice how its own process affects learning.

Start the sessionBack to desk

Opening question

Think of a small group that helped you learn. What did people do that made it work?

Collect behaviors, not personality traits. Translate “good chemistry” into actions the group can repeat.

Run of show

  1. 0-8 minutesOpening question and introductions.
  2. 8-18 minutesCreate working agreements.
  3. 18-38 minutesPractice with a low-stakes discussion task.
  4. 38-50 minutesObserve and repair the group process.
  5. 50-60 minutesFeedback, commitments, and close.

Working agreements

Participation

How will we make room for every voice without forcing disclosure?

Disagreement

How will we challenge an idea while protecting the person offering it?

Uncertainty

What will make it safe to say “I do not know”?

Repair

What should happen when the group drifts or someone is left out?

Practice the process

Give the group a low-stakes problem with more than one defensible answer. Ask one learner to observe only the process.

  1. Who entered the discussion first?
  2. Whose idea changed the group’s direction?
  3. What happened when people disagreed?
  4. What voice or perspective was missing?
Facilitator note

Do not solve the content problem for them. The learning target is how the group works while solving it.

Useful interventions

Widen

“We have heard two voices. Who sees this differently?”

Slow down

“What assumption are we making right now?”

Clarify

“Can someone restate that idea before responding?”

Repair

“We moved past that quickly. What did we miss?”

After class

  • Which agreement changed participation?
  • When did the group become more or less candid?
  • What intervention should you repeat next time?
